# CS & AI Mastery — v5.67

## Syntax-used teaching layer

Every executable example/editor covered by the universal line-by-line explainer now includes a compact **Syntax used** section when opened.

For each important construct that is actually present in the code, the section explains:

1. what the construct does,
2. its general syntax pattern,
3. how the current example uses it, and
4. why that construct is useful in this specific example.

The detector is language-aware and includes Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, C++/Java, shell, HTML, CSS, Dockerfile, JSON, and YAML constructs.

Important grounding rule: syntax is only shown when it is actually present. List comprehensions (`[...]`) and generator expressions (`(...)`) are distinguished explicitly.
