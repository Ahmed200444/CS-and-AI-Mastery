# KHDA Curriculum Benchmark — v5.47

This audit compares CS & AI Mastery against public curricula from Dubai training providers that appear in the KHDA training directory, then applies a **company-use-only gate**. A topic is added only when it maps to realistic professional work; certificate-only/theory-only coverage is not enough.

## Evidence rule

- **KHDA directory:** used to verify the institute/course-title context.
- **Institute syllabus:** used for detailed module/topic comparison when a public syllabus exists.
- A KHDA-listed institute does not mean every sentence on its marketing/syllabus page is separately KHDA-approved.

## Audit result

- Courses audited: **62 / 62**
- Lessons audited: **800**
- Affected lessons expanded: **49**
- Company-use concepts added: **89**
