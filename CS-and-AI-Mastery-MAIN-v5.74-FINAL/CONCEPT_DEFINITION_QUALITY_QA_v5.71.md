# Concept Definition Quality QA — v5.71

Scope checked:

- 62 course pages
- 800 lessons
- 3,427 key-concept entries

Quality rules:

1. No visible definition may use the old generic “is a key idea in…” / “It names the specific…” filler.
2. Recognized concepts use a dedicated definition.
3. When a lesson explanation actually discusses the named concept, that evidence is preferred for otherwise-unmapped concepts.
4. Unknown concepts receive a contextual explanation tied to the exact lesson and related concepts instead of copying one lesson sentence for every card.
5. Integrated application, edge/debug, and concept-transfer cards explain the learning goal and practical use rather than presenting meaningless pseudo-definitions.
6. Definitions may use multiple sentences when needed; simple concepts remain concise.
